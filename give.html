Where your gift goes

Gifts support our two program areas: leadership development and teaching ministry with churches across Canada, and the Love & Light relief and development work in South Asia — clean water, schooling, healthcare and vocational training for families.

Receipting & questions

We provide official income tax receipts for all donations made in Canada. Receipts are sent each February for donations made during the previous calendar year. For receipt questions, monthly giving changes, or gifts of securities, email info@idministries.ca.

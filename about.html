About us

Who we are

I.D. Ministries — Identity & Destiny — is a registered Canadian charity based in Grimsby, Ontario. We work with churches, ministry leaders and communities both at home in Canada and in the developing world.

Our mission

Our mission is to equip leaders to build healthy ministries intergenerationally.

Healthy ministries outlive the people who start them. We invest in leaders — pastors, boards, volunteers and the next generation coming up behind them — so that what they build keeps serving their communities long after the hand-off.

How we pursue it

Teach

Preaching, teaching and conference ministry with local churches across Canada.

Equip

Coaching and training for ministry leaders and boards, with a focus on succession and generational hand-off.

Serve

Humanitarian relief and development for families in South Asia through our Love & Light program.

Governance

I.D. Ministries is directed by a volunteer board of directors, serving without pay, which oversees the charity's finances, programs and Executive Director and reviews them regularly.

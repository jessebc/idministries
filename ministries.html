What we do

Our programs and services

I.D. Ministries carries out its charitable purpose through two ongoing program areas — leadership development with churches in Canada, and humanitarian relief and development overseas.

Canada

Leadership development & teaching ministry

Terry Bone travels to churches, conferences and ministry teams across Canada to teach, preach and train. The work is ongoing rather than event-based: alongside speaking, we coach pastors, leadership teams and boards through the practical work of building a ministry that stays healthy as it changes hands between generations.

  • Preaching and teaching in local churches across Canada.
  • Leadership training and conference sessions for ministry teams.
  • One-to-one coaching for pastors and board members.
  • Succession and intergenerational hand-off planning.

terrybone.global — operated by I.D. Ministries

South Asia

Love & Light — relief and development

For over twenty years, Love & Light has connected Canadian donors with proven local workers serving refugee camps and urban slums throughout South Asia. We fund established, locally-run programs rather than starting our own, so support reaches families through people their communities already trust.

  • Clean drinking water — well installation in underserved communities.
  • Primary education and safe learning environments for street children.
  • Education, residential care and mentorship for refugee children.
  • Vocational training and business skills for women in extreme poverty.
  • Prenatal care and nutrition support for mothers through a community clinic.
  • Resources and support for the local field workers who deliver it all.
